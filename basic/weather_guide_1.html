Intro

Why Talk About Weather?

Weather is one of the most common conversation topics in English-speaking countries. Being able to describe, ask about, and discuss weather conditions is an essential everyday skill — whether you're chatting with a neighbour, checking a weather forecast, or planning a trip.
🗣️ Small Talk
In many English-speaking cultures, especially British and American, weather is the most popular "small talk" topic. It's a safe, neutral subject you can use to start a conversation with anyone. Lovely day, isn't it? / Dreadful weather we're having!
📡 Practical Situations
You need weather vocabulary when listening to forecasts, reading travel guides, or planning outdoor activities. Knowing the right words makes you a more confident and natural English speaker. The forecast says it will be partly cloudy with a chance of showers.

Sunny

☀️ Sunny & Warm Weather

☀️
Clear Sky
Sunny
The sun is shining brightly and there are no clouds in the sky. It is a beautiful, clear day.
brightclearglorious
🌤️
Partly Cloudy
Partly Cloudy
There are some clouds in the sky, but the sun is still visible. The weather is pleasant.
pleasantmild
🥵
Extreme Heat
Scorching / Boiling
The temperature is extremely high. The air feels like an oven and everything is very hot to the touch.
scorchingboilingsweltering
📝 Key Adjectives for Sunny Weather
sunny · bright · clear · warm · hot · scorching · blazing · glorious · gorgeous · fine · lovely · fair · balmy · humid · dry
Example Sentences: It's a beautiful, sunny day. Let's go to the park. The weather is warm and clear today — perfect for a picnic. It's absolutely scorching outside. I need some water!
⚠️ Humidity vs. Dry Heat
Humid = hot and wet (sticky feeling)
Dry heat = hot without moisture
It's very humid today — my clothes feel damp. The desert has dry heat, not humidity.
03
Cloudy

☁️ Cloudy & Overcast Weather

☁️
Sky Covered
Cloudy
The sky is covered with clouds. The sun may still be faintly visible but the day feels grey.
greydullcloudy
🌫️
Completely Covered
Overcast
The entire sky is covered by thick grey clouds and no sunlight comes through. Very dull and depressing weather.
overcastgloomydreary
🌁
Low Visibility
Foggy / Misty
A thick layer of water vapour near the ground reduces visibility. Fog is very thick; mist is lighter.
foggymistyhazy
🔑 Cloudy vs. Overcast: "Cloudy" means there are many clouds, but you can still see some patches of blue sky. "Overcast" means the sky is completely covered — no blue at all. Overcast weather often leads to rain. It's quite cloudy, but I don't think it will rain. The sky is completely overcast — we should take an umbrella.
04
Rainy

🌧️ Rainy & Wet Weather

🌦️
Light Rain
Drizzle
Very light, fine rain that barely wets you. You might not even need an umbrella — just a light jacket.
lightfinemisty
🌧️
Moderate Rain
Shower
A brief period of rainfall that doesn't last long. Showers can come and go several times in a day.
briefscatteredintermittent
⛈️
Heavy Rain
Downpour / Torrential Rain
Very heavy, intense rainfall. You get completely soaked within seconds. Streets may flood quickly.
heavytorrentialpouring
🌧️ Rain Intensity — from light to heavy:
drizzle → light rain → shower → rain → heavy rain → downpour → torrential rain
Useful Rain Verbs:
to drizzle — It's drizzling outside.
to pour — It's pouring with rain!
to spit (BrE) — It's just spitting; don't worry.
to bucket down (informal) — It's bucketing down!
Common Rain Expressions: It's raining cats and dogs! (very heavy rain) We got caught in a shower on the way home. The rain is really coming down now.
05
Stormy

⛈️ Stormy & Windy Weather

💨
Moving Air
Windy / Breezy
Air moving at noticeable speed. A breeze is gentle and pleasant; wind is stronger and can affect movement.
breezygustywindy
🌪️
Extreme Wind
Gale / Hurricane
Dangerously strong winds. Gales can knock down trees; hurricanes cause massive destruction across large areas.
gale-forceviolentdestructive
⚡
Electrical Storm
Thunderstorm
Heavy rain accompanied by thunder (loud rumbling sound) and lightning (bright flashes of electricity in the sky).
thunderlightningsevere
⚡ Thunder vs. Lightning: Lightning is the flash of light (electricity) you see in the sky. Thunder is the loud rumbling sound you hear afterwards. Light travels faster than sound, so you always see lightning before you hear thunder. I can see lightning in the distance — there must be a storm coming. That thunder was so loud it shook the windows!
💨 Wind Vocabulary — from gentle to violent:
breath of air → breeze → gusty wind → strong wind → gale → storm → hurricane / typhoon / cyclone
06
Cold

❄️ Cold, Frosty & Snowy Weather

🥶
Low Temperature
Cold / Freezing
The temperature is very low. "Freezing" is stronger than "cold" — it means it's at or below 0°C / 32°F.
chillycoldfreezing
❄️
Frozen Water
Frost / Ice
When temperatures fall below 0°C, water freezes. Frost appears as thin ice crystals on surfaces in the morning.
icyfrostyslippery
🌨️
Snowfall
Snow / Blizzard
Snow falls when temperatures are at or below 0°C. A blizzard is a severe snowstorm with strong winds and very low visibility.
snowypowderyblizzard
❄️ Cold Weather Vocabulary:
chilly — slightly cold, uncomfortable
nippy (informal) — pleasantly cool/cold
bitter/biting cold — extremely cold and unpleasant
sub-zero — temperatures below 0°C
wintry — cold and snowy, typical of winter
🌨️ Snow Vocabulary:
snowfall — snow that falls from the sky
snowflake — a single crystal of snow
sleet — a mix of rain and snow
hail — small balls of ice that fall like rain
black ice — invisible thin ice on roads (dangerous) The roads are covered in black ice — drive carefully!

🗣️ Starting a Weather Conversation
What's the weather like today? — Most common question
Lovely day, isn't it? — British small talk
Terrible weather, isn't it? — Complaining politely
Have you seen the forecast? — Asking about predictions
☀️ Talking About Good Weather
What gorgeous weather we're having!
It's absolutely glorious outside.
Make the most of the sunshine while it lasts.
I love this warm weather — it really lifts your spirits.
🌧️ Talking About Bad Weather
It's dreadful weather today, isn't it?
I'm absolutely soaked — I forgot my umbrella.
It's been raining cats and dogs all morning.
The weather has really taken a turn for the worse.
🌨️ Talking About Extreme Weather
They've issued a weather warning for the area.
The storm caused a lot of damage.
We're under a freeze warning tonight.
The roads are treacherous in this weather.

💡 Study Tips for Weather Vocabulary

  • Listen to English weather forecasts on TV or radio — BBC Weather, CNN, or The Weather Channel are great.
  • Write a short "weather diary" every day in English — describe what the weather is like outside.
  • Notice how native speakers talk about weather: learn their idiomatic expressions, not just single words.
  • Group vocabulary by weather type (sunny words, rainy words, cold words) rather than learning in isolation.
  • Practice saying the weather when you look outside — make it a daily habit!

Exercise 3 — Describe the Weather

Write 2–3 sentences describing the weather in each situation. Use vocabulary from this guide.

🏖️ Situation 1: It's summer, 38°C, no clouds, very humid.

Situation 2: Conditions in the mountains are severe. A blizzard is in progress with heavy snowfall and gale-force winds. Temperatures are well below freezing at −15°C — dangerously cold and icy.

Situation 3: It's a mild spring morning with a pleasant breeze. The sky is partly cloudy, and there are occasional light showers passing through, but it should brighten up later.
